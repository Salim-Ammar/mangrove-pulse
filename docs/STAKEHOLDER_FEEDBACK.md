# Stakeholder review of the inspection briefs

Proposed process | 2 October 2026 | Reviews completed: 0

## Question to test

Does an intended monitoring practitioner understand the brief and use it to choose a useful next evidence-gathering action? This tests workflow relevance/usability, not 2018 land-cover accuracy, ecological diagnosis or drone feasibility.

Seek feedback from at least three distinct, relevant people. Suitable perspectives include a mangrove/ecology practitioner, an EO or GIS analyst and a person responsible for monitoring or field surveys. These are target roles, not confirmed contacts or partners. Verify any organiser-specific eligibility/evidence rules before claiming a validation bonus.

## Suggested invitation (not sent)

Hello, we are the Mangrove Pulse team. We have a small satellite-evidence PoC and three historical Khor Kalba inspection briefs. Could you spend about 10 minutes reviewing one brief and telling us whether it helps you decide what evidence to seek next? The cases remain unresolved, and the amphibious drone is only proposed. We are seeking practical criticism, not endorsement. May we record your feedback, and would you prefer to be identified by role only?

## Neutral review task

Share one [inspection brief](CASES.md) and its map. Before explaining it, ask the practitioner to describe the location, review reason, what is known and what remains uncertain. Then ask:

1. Does this resemble a decision you make? How do you handle it today?
2. What does the brief let you decide, if anything? What information is missing or confusing?
3. What evidence would you request first, and why? What would make you reject or defer this case?
4. If current field observations were needed, which method would you use? Would existing imagery, a survey, a boat, a standard drone or another approach suffice?
5. What change would make this brief more useful? What specific constraint could prevent its use?

Avoid presenting the drone as the expected answer or asking whether the idea is good. Accept rejection, unresolved responses and requests for a simpler alternative. Agreement to review establishes neither partnership, field-access permission nor endorsement.

## Evidence record

Keep contact details and raw correspondence outside the public repository. Record consent and any sharing restrictions. A public summary should use anonymous IDs/roles unless identification and quotations are explicitly permitted.

For each completed review, record the date/method, relevant role, brief/version, decision or action proposed, concrete criticism, missing evidence, requested changes, consent and unresolved issues. Separate quotations from the team's interpretation and retain the underlying evidence privately. Responses must come from actual reviews, not prefilled examples.
