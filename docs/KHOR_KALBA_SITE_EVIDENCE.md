# Khor Kalba: evidence for the inspection concept

Reviewed 7 October 2026. Published site context, not new team observations or validation of the frozen 2018 map.

## What is genuinely local

The [official Ramsar site record](https://rsis.ramsar.org/RISapp/files/RISrep/AE2125RIS.pdf), completed 1 September 2012, page 5, reports historical water-property gradients and tidal exchange. It motivates a separate dated contact-sensing question without establishing present harm, planting need or a required aircraft. The [water-contact rationale](WATER_CONTACT_RATIONALE.md) connects that question to candidate EO data, matched alternatives and analytical rejection gates. Current water observations remain absent.

The [UAE soil-carbon field report](https://mangrovealliance4climate.org/media/ygbbgmcv/uae-mangroves-annual-carbon-sequestration-final-report.pdf) documents two Khor Kalba sites, KK1 and KK2, sampled on 5 September 2019. Table 1 gives their coordinates and describes both as healthy. Its method used sediment cores and laboratory analysis. Those descriptions apply to two sites at their observation date, not to our three cases or the reserve today.

The report establishes a local measurement precedent, but supplies neither planting need, 2018 cell labels, a water-probe payload requirement nor evidence of benefits from our aircraft. Conductivity/temperature observations cannot replace its sediment/laboratory carbon measurements.

The [Coastal Ecosystem Mapping and Media Viability report](https://gefblueforests.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/The-Coastal-Ecosystem-Mapping-and-Media-Viability-Project_Final-6.21.2016-1.pdf) describes drone imagery collected at Khor Kalba and Ras Al Khor in September 2015. Its methodology uses photogrammetry and GIS. The limitations section warns that its canopy-volume approach overestimates woody biomass and cannot directly supply reliable carbon estimates without appropriate field relationships.

Drone-based mangrove imagery at this site has precedents. Our proposed contribution is a traceable satellite-question-to-observation handoff and, if separately justified and tested, task-specific supported contact sensing. Neither currently demonstrates an operational advantage.

## Which date answers which question?

| Question | Suitable evidence | Present status |
|---|---|---|
| Was the November 2018 cover prediction correct? | Independent sufficiently detailed near-date evidence, appropriate labels, alignment and uncertainty | Unresolved; three-point October 2018 imagery metadata is not a labelled validation dataset |
| What could a current inspection investigate? | Refreshed current EO, qualified task definition and dated current observations | Proposed; older reports inform the protocol, not present conditions |
| Is the amphibious platform better? | Matched accepted-quality observations and measured whole-mission resources/safeguards | Unproven; local precedent and hypothetical energy do not establish superiority |

## Reference-source screening

The [2020 Northern Emirates mapping paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/environmental-science/articles/10.3389/fenvs.2020.00102/full) is contextual literature, not accepted historical truth. Its stated 2019 QuickBird/EO-1 acquisitions conflict with mission timelines: [NASA's QuickBird archive](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/quickbird-level-1b-panchromatic-satellite-imagery) ends in January 2015, and [NASA's EO-1 notice](https://www.nasa.gov/technology/nasa-says-goodbye-to-a-pathfinder-earth-satellite-after-17-years/) gives shutdown in March 2017. Scene identifiers or corrected metadata would be needed before trusting those acquisition dates. We do not transfer its accuracy scores to this PoC.

The [PANGAEA 2017 mangrove distribution dataset](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.884814) has explicit CC-BY-3.0 licensing and downloadable tabular data. It is a different-year Landsat-derived distribution product, not independent fine-detail 2018 field labels; exact AOI applicability and reference lineage have not been assessed here.

Wayback remains a potential visual-review source subject to rights, dates and alignment checks. [Esri's export guidance](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/arcgis-living-atlas/imagery/wayback-export) requires an organizational account and limits exported tiles to ArcGIS use; public viewability is not blanket permission to bundle pixels in Colab/GitHub. No tiles were exported or redistributed.

## Decision

Retain the frozen 2018 demonstration and use the 2015/2019 reports as dated local precedents. Independent accuracy assessment requires lawful near-date reference evidence before sampling/label estimation; a current-date study would be a separate experiment. The original experiment's dates, threshold and labels remain unchanged. The [three-claim protocols](VALIDATION_AND_BENEFIT_TESTS.md) remain proposed, with empty registers.
